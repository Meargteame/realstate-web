## 🎨 Design System

### Breakpoints:
```

sm:  576-767px (Large phones)
md:  768-991px (Tablets)
lg:  992-1199px (Small laptops)
xl:  1200-1599px (Laptops)
xxl: ≥ 1600px  (Large desktops)
```

### Spacing Scale:
```
Mobile:  16px padding, 12px gaps, 24px margins
Tablet:  24px padding, 16px gaps, 24px margins
Desktop: 32-48px padding, 24-32px gaps, 32px margins

### Typography Scale:
```
Mobile:  Title 24px, Heading 18px, Body 14px, Small 12px
